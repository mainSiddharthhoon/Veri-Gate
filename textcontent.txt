4. THE PROBLEM — VERIFICATION CHALLENGES & FRAUD RISKS
================================================================================
Eyebrow: VERIFICATION CHALLENGES • FRAUD RISKS
Heading: Modern identity fraud exploits gaps between isolated checks.
Lead Paragraph:
  Basic visual inspection and standalone OCR frequently miss altered dates,
  localized image splicing, and mismatched portrait pairs. VeriGate
  cross-correlates deterministic rules, compression forensics, and biometric
  comparison into a unified analysis.

Threat Cards:
  Card 01:
    Badge: CRITICAL 01
    Pill: DIGITAL TAMPERING
    Title: Localized Splicing
    Description: Digital cut-and-paste modifications altering dates, names, or numbers while attempting to preserve surrounding document textures.
    Footer Indicator: DETECTION: ELA FORENSICS

  Card 02:
    Badge: CRITICAL 02
    Pill: FACE MISMATCH
    Title: Portrait Substitution
    Description: Discrepancies between the photo printed on the identity document and the live presented person, requiring vector-level feature comparison.
    Footer Indicator: DETECTION: BIOMETRICS

  Card 03:
    Badge: HIGH 03
    Pill: EXPIRED & INVALID DATES
    Title: Date Inconsistencies
    Description: Expired credentials, future dates of birth, or issuance dates occurring after expiration that bypass simple text extraction.
    Footer Indicator: DETECTION: DETERMINISTIC RULES

  Card 04:
    Badge: HIGH 04
    Pill: MRZ CHECKSUM ERRORS
    Title: Check Digit Flaws
    Description: Forged passport or travel document numbers that fail mathematical check-digit algorithms encoded in machine-readable zones.
    Footer Indicator: DETECTION: MRZ ALGORITHMS

  Card 05:
    Badge: HIGH 05
    Pill: UNQUALIFIED INPUTS
    Title: Defective Submissions
    Description: Low-resolution, blurry, heavily cropped, or non-identity images that degrade downstream extraction and comparison models.
    Footer Indicator: DETECTION: INPUT QUALIFICATION

  Card 06:
    Badge: ELEVATED 06
    Pill: UNEXPLAINED VERDICTS
    Title: Opaque Black-Box Systems
    Description: Siloed checks that evaluate OCR, dates, and images in isolation without cross-referencing findings into a unified, evidence-based assessment.
    Footer Indicator: DETECTION: GEMMA REASONING
