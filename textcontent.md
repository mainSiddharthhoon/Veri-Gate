### 4. The Problem — Verification Challenges & Fraud Risks
- **Eyebrow:** `VERIFICATION CHALLENGES • FRAUD RISKS`
- **Heading:** `Modern identity fraud exploits gaps between isolated checks.`
- **Lead Paragraph:**  
  *Basic visual inspection and standalone OCR frequently miss altered dates, localized image splicing, and mismatched portrait pairs. VeriGate cross-correlates deterministic rules, compression forensics, and biometric comparison into a unified analysis.*

#### Verification Threat Cards:
1. **Critical 01 — Localized Splicing**  
   - *Pill:* `DIGITAL TAMPERING`  
   - *Description:* Digital cut-and-paste modifications altering dates, names, or numbers while attempting to preserve surrounding document textures.  
   - *Footer Indicator:* `DETECTION: ELA FORENSICS`
2. **Critical 02 — Portrait Substitution**  
   - *Pill:* `FACE MISMATCH`  
   - *Description:* Discrepancies between the photo printed on the identity document and the live presented person, requiring vector-level feature comparison.  
   - *Footer Indicator:* `DETECTION: BIOMETRICS`
3. **High 03 — Date Inconsistencies**  
   - *Pill:* `EXPIRED & INVALID DATES`  
   - *Description:* Expired credentials, future dates of birth, or issuance dates occurring after expiration that bypass simple text extraction.  
   - *Footer Indicator:* `DETECTION: DETERMINISTIC RULES`
4. **High 04 — Check Digit Flaws**  
   - *Pill:* `MRZ CHECKSUM ERRORS`  
   - *Description:* Forged passport or travel document numbers that fail mathematical check-digit algorithms encoded in machine-readable zones.  
   - *Footer Indicator:* `DETECTION: MRZ ALGORITHMS`
5. **High 05 — Defective Submissions**  
   - *Pill:* `UNQUALIFIED INPUTS`  
   - *Description:* Low-resolution, blurry, heavily cropped, or non-identity images that degrade downstream extraction and comparison models.  
   - *Footer Indicator:* `DETECTION: INPUT QUALIFICATION`
6. **Elevated 06 — Opaque Black-Box Systems**  
   - *Pill:* `UNEXPLAINED VERDICTS`  
   - *Description:* Siloed checks that evaluate OCR, dates, and images in isolation without cross-referencing findings into a unified, evidence-based assessment.  
   - *Footer Indicator:* `DETECTION: GEMMA REASONING`

1. **Pre-Flight Input Qualification**  
   - *Badge:* `PRE-FLIGHT QUALIFICATION` | *Index:* `01 / 06`  
   - *Subtitle:* Gemma visual inspection verifies document and selfie suitability  
   - *Narrative:* Before running downstream extraction, Gemma inspects both uploaded images to confirm document visibility, detect image category, and ensure a clear, usable frontal portrait is present.  
   - *Tags:* `IMAGE SUITABILITY` | `FACE VISIBILITY` | `FAIL-EARLY`  
   - *Metric:* `INPUT: QUALIFIED` | `STAGE 01 GATEWAY`
2. **OCR & Field Extraction**  
   - *Badge:* `DOCUMENT OCR` | *Index:* `02 / 06`  
   - *Subtitle:* Reading text fields from document surface  
   - *Narrative:* Extracts textual identity credentials including full name, document number, date of birth, expiration date, and issuing authority for downstream rule checking.  
   - *Tags:* `FIELD EXTRACTION` | `TEXT NORMALIZATION` | `TYPOGRAPHIC SCAN`  
   - *Metric:* `EXTRACTION: STRUCTURED` | `DATA ENGINE`
3. **MRZ Check Digit Verification**  
   - *Badge:* `MRZ PARITY` | *Index:* `03 / 06`  
   - *Subtitle:* Mathematical check-digit validation when MRZ is present  
   - *Narrative:* When an MRZ is present on passports or machine-readable IDs, VeriGate recalculates modular check digits on document numbers, dates of birth, and expiration dates.  
   - *Tags:* `CONDITIONAL MRZ` | `MOD-7 CHECK DIGITS` | `COMPOSITE INTEGRITY`  
   - *Metric:* `MRZ: VERIFIED` | `CONDITIONAL AUDIT`
4. **Deterministic Document Validation**  
   - *Badge:* `DETERMINISTIC RULE LAYER` | *Index:* `04 / 06`  
   - *Subtitle:* Mathematical checks on dates, ages, and chronological consistency  
   - *Narrative:* Evaluates explicit business logic: confirms DOB is in the past, computes applicant calendar age, validates that issuance predates expiration, and flags expired credentials.  
   - *Tags:* `CALENDAR LOGIC` | `AGE COMPUTATION` | `EXPIRY EVALUATION`  
   - *Metric:* `RULES: DETERMINISTIC` | `ZERO HALLUCINATIONS`
5. **Tampering Forensics (ELA)**  
   - *Badge:* `COMPRESSION ANALYSIS` | *Index:* `05 / 06`  
   - *Subtitle:* Error Level Analysis detecting image resave differentials  
   - *Narrative:* Re-compresses the document image at fixed JPEG quality levels and computes residual pixel error maps to reveal localized splicing, cloned fields, or font tampering.  
   - *Tags:* `ERROR LEVEL ANALYSIS` | `SPLICING DETECTION` | `PIXEL RESIDUALS`  
   - *Metric:* `FORENSICS: ELA METRICS` | `PIXEL RESIDUALS`
6. **Biometric Matching & Gemma Reasoning**

   - *Tags:* `FACIAL EMBEDDINGS` | `COSINE SIMILARITY` | `GEMMA ARBITER`

#### Results Console (Right Column):
- **Standby State:**
  - *Title:* Awaiting Evidence Injection
  - *Description:* Attach an identity document and facial portrait on the left console, then trigger Start Screening to activate multimodal verification.
  - *Telemetry Chips:* `INPUT QUALIFICATION`, `OCR & MRZ KERNEL`, `ELA FORENSICS`, `BIOMETRIC COMPARISON`, `GEMMA ARBITER`
- **Active Processing State:**
  - Dynamic progress ring & bar (0% - 100%)
  - 7-Stage Live Execution Tracker:
    1. Input Qualification
    2. OCR & Field Extraction
    3. MRZ Validation
    4. Deterministic Validation
    5. Tampering Forensics
    6. Face Verification
    7. Gemma AI Reasoning
- **Dynamic Evidence Intelligence Report:**
  1. **Final Decision Banner:** Overall Decision (`APPROVE`, `REVIEW`, `REJECT`) + Risk Level (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `CRITICAL`) + Risk Score (`0–100`)
  2. **AI Verdict:** One-line evidence-grounded summary
  3. **Identity Credential Details:** Extracted Full Name, Document Number, Nationality, Date of Birth, Calculated Subject Age, Expiry Date
  4. **Document Status:** Overall Document Status (`VALID` / `INVALID`)
  5. **Deterministic Validation Checks:** Required Fields, Date of Birth Past Check, Expiry Status Check, Issue Before Expiry, MRZ Check Digits (Passed, Failed, or Not Applicable)
  6. **Face Verification:** Match Status (`MATCH` / `MISMATCH` / `INCONCLUSIVE`), Cosine Distance vs Acceptance Threshold
  7. **Tampering Forensics (ELA):** Compression Variance Score, Flagged Suspicious Regions, Forensics Residual Heatmap
  8. **Date Intelligence & Chronology:** Verification Date, Subject Age, Expiry Status, Chronological Inconsistencies
  9. **Reference & Issuance Record Check:** Reference Database Status & Matching Result
  10. **Detected Risk Factors:** Specific triggered anomalies list (or "Zero risk anomalies detected")
  11. **Session Metadata:** Session ID, Gemma Arbiter Model, Processing Latency
  12. **AI Reasoning Narrative:** Multi-factor synthesis explaining the evidence and why the decision and score were reached.
  13. **Action:** `Run Another Screening`
