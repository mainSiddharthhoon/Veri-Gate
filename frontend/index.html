VERIFICATION CHALLENGES • FRAUD RISKS

Modern identity fraud exploits gaps between isolated checks.

Basic visual inspection and standalone OCR frequently miss altered dates, localized image splicing, and mismatched portrait pairs. VeriGate cross-correlates deterministic rules, compression forensics, and biometric comparison into a unified analysis.

CRITICAL 01 DIGITAL TAMPERING

Localized Splicing

Digital cut-and-paste modifications altering dates, names, or numbers while attempting to preserve surrounding document textures.

DETECTION: ELA FORENSICS
CRITICAL 02 FACE MISMATCH

Portrait Substitution

Discrepancies between the photo printed on the identity document and the live presented person, requiring vector-level feature comparison.

DETECTION: BIOMETRICS
HIGH 03 EXPIRED & INVALID DATES

Date Inconsistencies

Expired credentials, future dates of birth, or issuance dates occurring after expiration that bypass simple text extraction.

DETECTION: DETERMINISTIC RULES
HIGH 04 MRZ CHECKSUM ERRORS

Check Digit Flaws

Forged passport or travel document numbers that fail mathematical check-digit algorithms encoded in machine-readable zones.

DETECTION: MRZ ALGORITHMS
HIGH 05 UNQUALIFIED INPUTS

Defective Submissions

Low-resolution, blurry, heavily cropped, or non-identity images that degrade downstream extraction and comparison models.
